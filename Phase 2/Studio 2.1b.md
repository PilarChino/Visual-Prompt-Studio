Printmaking

Create an artwork that uses Relief Printmaking and emphasizes lines, contrast, movement, and texture. Use carving tools such as gouges, V-cutters, and U-cutters on a linoleum or wood block, along with the processes characteristic of this medium: carving away negative space, applying ink to the raised surfaces with a brayer, and transferring the image to paper through pressure. The composition should feature Odile, the Black Swan from Swan Lake, shown from the waist up with dramatic black feathers spreading outward like wings. Bold carved lines should define the figure, feathers, and flowing movement, while strong black-and-white contrast creates emphasis and visual impact. The texture should be created through expressive carved marks, cross-hatching, and patterned cuts that suggest layered feathers and motion. The final print should have a dramatic, theatrical quality, showcasing the distinctive graphic appearance and high contrast of relief printing.
