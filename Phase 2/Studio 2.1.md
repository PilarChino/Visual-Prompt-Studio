Oil Painting 
Create an artwork that uses Oil painting and emphasizes lines, contrast, movement, and texture. Use a palette knife, stiff bristle brushes, fine detail brushes, and layered oil paints with linseed oil as the binder, along with the techniques of glazing, impasto, slow drying, and color blending characteristic of this medium. The composition should feature Odile, the Black Swan from Swan Lake, shown from the waist up beneath a dramatic spotlight. Flowing black feathers surround the dancer and blend into expressive brushstrokes, creating a powerful sense of movement. Strong directional lines formed by the arms, neck, and feathers guide the viewer’s eye, while dramatic light against a dark background creates intense contrast. Thick textured layers of paint emphasize the feathers and costume, showcasing the rich texture and depth unique to oil painting.
